What’s working

  • Waste is tight: $9,959 in actionable zero-order search-term spend, 0.9% of $1,093,655.
  • Exact carries 61.5% of Sponsored Products spend at a 19.84% CVR. Targeting is deliberate, not discovery-led.
  • Top of Search takes 56.7% of SP spend at a 24.05% CVR and 29.7% ACoS, the best-converting placement.
  • Brand defense is efficient: $39,404 (3.6% of SP spend) at 17.7% ACoS.

What to fix

  • Sep 2026 vs Sep 2025: ad spend +63.2%, ad sales +26.5%, total sales +7.2%. TACoS rose from 11.7% to 17.8%.
  • CPC is up 24.5% year over year while CVR fell 1.9 pts (18.48% to 16.6%). Each incremental dollar buys less.
  • 1,295 of 1,390 enabled SP campaigns were in budget under 85% of the window; Amazon estimates $63,266-$190,562 in missed sales on 140 of them.
  • 65.74% of ad spend sits in campaigns running above 30% ACoS.
