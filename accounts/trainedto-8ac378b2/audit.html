What’s working

  • Brand demand is strong: 18,598 searches a month on the Sidekick name, converting on paid clicks at 21.32%.
  • Account ACoS is 11.7% on $9,626 of spend, returning $82,370 in ad sales.
  • Top of Search returns 85.5% of ad sales on 58.8% of spend at 8.0% ACoS.
  • September 2026 total sales rose 10.4% YoY and orders 15.5%, with TACoS holding at 4.5%.

What to fix

  • Sidekick wins only 28.0% of purchases on 'axis board' (5,860 searches), its own product name, despite an Exact bid; lookalikes take the rest.
  • On 'sidekick', another SIDEKICK brand owns the headline ad, and 6 brand-name queries have no Exact target.
  • Generic demand is unaddressed: Sidekick holds 0.16% purchase share on 'balance board' (81,500 monthly searches) with no paid coverage.
  • 11 of 12 selling ASINs have no ads, including the Echo scraper ($20,516 in 30 days) and the new night splint (B0HMJQ6Y8Y).
  • Product Pages take 36.2% of spend at 51.2% ACoS, and the only campaign stays within budget for just 63.3% of the window going into Q4.

Recommendations

Efficiency Execute Priority: Low

Observation. Account ACoS is 11.7%: $9,626 of spend returning $82,370 in ad sales over the last 30 days. No target ACoS was supplied, so this is descriptive rather than pass/fail.

Diagnosis. The efficiency comes from demand Meta has already created. 65.3% of spend sits on Sidekick brand-name keywords at 11.5% ACoS. The other 34.7% sits at 12.1% ACoS and is almost entirely the AxisBoard product name, not category search.

Recommendation. Set three ACoS targets: brand name, AxisBoard product name, and category growth. At 11.7% blended, there is room to fund real category acquisition and still run a profitable account.

TACoS vs Objective Client Conversation Priority: Low

Observation. TACoS is 4.5%: ad spend equals 4.5% of $212,850 in total sales across 2,416 orders. It has held between 4.0% and 5.2% every month since November 2025.

Diagnosis. Amazon is catching demand created elsewhere, not creating its own. Peak sales month was August 2025 at $262,621, with $0 in ad spend.

Recommendation. Agree a TACoS ceiling tied to the 2027 revenue goal. Use the headroom above 4.5% to build non-branded and new-line demand on Amazon, so growth does not depend on Meta alone.

Brand Defense Execute Priority: High

Observation. Brand-name spend ('sidekick' terms) is $6,290, 65.3% of Sponsored Products spend, returning $54,717 at 11.5% ACoS on 18,598 monthly searches. 6 brand-name queries have no Exact target, including 'sidekick' (3,002 searches, 65.0% purchase share) and 'sidekick tool' (1,436 searches, 56.91%). AxisBoard terms are now treated as non-branded because competitors use the name: on 'axis board' (5,860 searches) Sidekick wins only 28.0% of purchases despite an Exact bid.

Diagnosis. Live searches on October 19 show why. On 'axis board', Sidekick holds the first sponsored slot and four lower-priced lookalikes take the next four, two of them titled 'Axis Board'. On 'sidekick', the headline ad and the first sponsored results belong to an unrelated comic-storage brand also called SIDEKICK. No Sponsored Brands ad protects either page, and nothing defends Sidekick's own product pages.

Recommendation. Close the perimeter: Sponsored Brands headlines on 'axis board', 'sidekick axis board' and 'sidekick tool', Exact targets for the 6 uncovered brand-name queries, and Sponsored Display / product targeting on Sidekick's own ASINs. Report the 'Axis Board' title use through Brand Registry, and pair it with A+ and brand-story content that shows why the original costs more.

Search Opportunities Execute Priority: High

Observation. 6 non-branded queries pass the signal filter. 5 show organic purchases with no paid coverage, such as 'ankle balance board' (3,131 searches, 5.16% purchase share) and 'balance board' (81,500 searches, 0.16%). The sixth, 'axis board', is covered by Exact: 5,860 searches, 256 paid orders at 11.5% ACoS, but only a 28.0% purchase share.

Diagnosis. The only non-branded term the account funds is its own product name, now contested by lookalikes. True category demand gets no paid coverage of any kind: on 'balance board', Sidekick takes 4 purchases from 81,500 monthly searches.

Recommendation. Defend 'axis board' with Sponsored Brands and Top of Search bids, then launch Exact tests on the 5 category queries with capped budgets, plus Auto and Phrase to surface audience terms such as athletes and gymnasts. Promote converters into CAT_SP campaigns once they clear the category ACoS target.

Strategic Intent Mix Execute Priority: Low

Observation. Brand Defense ('sidekick' terms) carries 65.3% of ad spend at 11.5% ACoS. Category & Generic carries 34.7% ($3,336 at 12.1% ACoS, $27,654 in sales), but nearly all of it is the AxisBoard product name. Offensive, Prospecting and Retargeting are all at 0%.

Diagnosis. Reclassifying 'axis board' as non-branded shows a real number, but not real category acquisition: the generic side is a few Exact keywords around 'ankle board', with no Auto, Phrase or product targeting to find more. There is no competitor conquesting against the knockoffs and no Sponsored Display remarketing.

Recommendation. Rebuild the mix in three layers before Q4 peaks: category Exact and Phrase per product line (AxisBoard, scrapers, Heel Hero, injury rehab) with its own ACoS target, ASIN targeting on knockoff and competitor listings, and Sponsored Display remarketing to bring back product-page visitors.
