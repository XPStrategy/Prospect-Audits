1. Overall

Observation
Account sales went from $36K in January 2025 to a $263K peak in August 2025 after the AxisBoard launch, and have held at $167K-$213K a month in 2026. Conversion sits at 16-19% and the buy box at 98-100%. In Balance Boards (about $3.4M a month in August), Sidekick's estimated share fell from 4.9% in August 2025 to 4.0% in August 2026, while the subcategory grew 9% Jan-Aug.
Diagnosis
The brand is held up by branded demand ('sidekick axis board' 12.6K searches, up 4x) rather than category demand: it ranks #27 on 'balance board' (67K) and is absent from 'wobble board', 'balance pad' and 'slant board' (104K combined), where its own Heel Hero competes. 'axis board' (11.3K, #1 organic) is a contested product-name term, not a niche Sidekick owns: lookalikes use the name in their titles, and in September 2026 Search Query Performance data Sidekick won only 28.0% of purchases on 'axis board' (5,860 searches) despite an Exact bid. The Echo scraper line is shrinking (-21% trailing 12 months) and the Q4 peak went to gift-led brands.
