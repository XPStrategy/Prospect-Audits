What’s working

  • Blended efficiency is already on plan: Account ACoS is 19.8% against your 20% ceiling and TACoS sits at 10.6%, so the account is not overspending on advertising in aggregate.
  • Advertising is disciplined: only 5.8% of search-term spend ($4,439) and 3.9% of ASIN-target spend reached zero-order targets, so waste is relatively contained.
  • The hero net converts exceptionally at a 20.24% conversion rate, and Brand Defense runs at a 10.8% ACoS, so demand for the flagship and the brand name is being captured efficiently.
  • Top of Search is well funded and productive: 54.8% of spend sits there at a 12.93% conversion rate and an 18.2% ACoS.

What to fix

  • Budget starvation is the largest hidden leak: 217 of 290 enabled Sponsored Products campaigns with spend run out of budget before the day ends, and Amazon estimates $9,453–$29,118 in missed sales over 30 days on the 52 campaigns it flags. The first choked off is a profitable long-tail Soccer Net line, in budget only 20.1% of the time at a 17.8% ACoS.
  • Proven demand is under-controlled: Auto drives 39.9% of Sponsored Products sales ($160,011) but only 616 Exact-match keywords are live, and 977 converting Auto queries ($124,122, 77.6% of Auto sales) have no Exact keyword — so bids and CPCs on your best queries run on autopilot.
  • Search coverage has open doors: 12 non-branded queries already convert in Auto and 8 more through manual or Sponsored Brands targeting, all with no Exact coverage — including the competitor term 'bownet' at 2,369 monthly searches, 27.03% purchase share and 17 orders at a 5.9% blended ACoS. That is profitable share captured by accident rather than by design.
  • The flat headline hides erosion: year over year the same ad spend produced 11.2% fewer ad sales at a higher ACoS (14.5% to 16.1%). The account is working harder to hold the line, which is the plateau you described, quantified.

TACoS vs Objective Client Conversation Priority: Low

Observation. TACoS is 10.6% against a 10% profit-first target, on $879,281 in total sales and 8,875 orders in the window, 0.6 points above plan.

Diagnosis. TACoS is only marginally above plan; the lever is where the spend sits, not how much of it there is.

Recommendation. TACoS is 10.6% versus the 10% target. The priority is reallocating inefficient spend toward proven efficient campaigns and targets before considering any aggregate spend reduction.

Keyword Harvesting Execute Priority: High

Observation. Automatic targeting drives 32.2% of Sponsored Products spend and 39.9% of its sales ($160,011); Product Targeting adds a further 17.4% of spend and 14.3% of sales. Only 616 Exact-match keywords are live, and 977 converting Auto queries — worth $124,122, or 77.6% of Auto sales — have no Exact-match keyword.

Diagnosis. Auto is the single largest source of Sponsored Products sales, but the account discovers winning queries there and rarely graduates them to controlled Exact targeting, so bids and CPCs on proven demand are set by the auction rather than by you.

Recommendation. Harvest the 977 converting Auto queries into structured Exact-match campaigns with deliberate bids for bid isolation, budget control, and match-type-specific optimization — reviewed by hand rather than left to a black-box tool.

Brand Defense Execute Priority: High

Observation. Branded spend is $4,277 (5.6% of Sponsored Products spend), driving $39,658 in ad sales at 10.8% ACoS. Including Sponsored Brands, branded activity is 5.4% of SP+SB spend. Branded search demand: 11,769 queries in the reporting month. 4 high-volume branded queries lack Exact-match coverage. Live SERP evidence was not captured in this run.

Recommendation. Strengthen Brand Defense: add Exact-match targets for the coverage-gap queries, verify bid strength on high-volume branded keywords, and review competitor Sponsored placements on SERP.

Search Opportunities Execute Priority: High

Observation. 94 non-branded queries pass the signal filter. 12 already convert in Automatic campaigns with no Exact-match coverage, 8 convert through manual or Sponsored Brands targeting with no Exact, 20 show organic demand only, and 1 converts inefficiently. The clearest example is the competitor term 'bownet' at 2,369 monthly searches and 27.03% purchase share: it has paid coverage but no Exact, and generated 17 orders at a 5.9% blended ACoS.

Diagnosis. The account is winning valuable and adjacent-category demand, including pitching screens, soccer goals and bags, by accident, so CPCs and rank on those queries are uncontrolled and exposed to any competitor who targets them deliberately.

Recommendation. Build Exact-match campaigns for the 12 Auto-converting and 8 paid-converting queries first for bid isolation and budget control; test the 20 organic-only queries with modest budgets; and fix the 1 inefficient query with negatives before scaling.

Negative Keyword Review Execute Priority: Medium

Observation. 181 recurring search-term fragments meet the inefficiency floor, showing either zero orders or persistently high ACoS. Because the same query is counted across its 1-, 2- and 3-word fragments, the raw fragment total ($16,243) is non-additive; the deduplicated unique search-term spend touching at least one flagged fragment is $4,925.

Diagnosis. A share of search-term spend flows to phrases that consistently fail to convert, dragging efficiency on otherwise sound campaigns.

Recommendation. Review the top fragments as manual negative candidates one campaign at a time, never as blanket account negatives, so seasonal terms that only convert in certain months are preserved.

Budget Opportunity Client Conversation Priority: High

Observation. 217 of 290 enabled Sponsored Products campaigns with spend were in budget less than 85% of the reporting window. Amazon reports estimated missed sales on 52 campaigns, totaling approximately $9,453–$29,118 over 30 days.

Diagnosis. Efficient campaigns are throttled before the day ends; the most-starved is a profitable Soccer Net line in budget only 20.1% of the time at a 17.8% ACoS. This also explains why raising the daily account cap during your budget test did not move revenue: the money had no un-capped, efficient campaign to flow into.

Recommendation. Reset budgets product-first, prioritized by Estimated Missed Sales, and move to portfolio-level pacing so proven campaigns stop running dry. This is the fastest profit recovery on the account, and it needs stated inventory support per SKU before scaling.

ASIN Conversion Opportunity Client Conversation Priority: Medium

Observation. The top 50 ASINs drive 84.7% of total sales. Median conversion among the top 10 is 5.43% at a 20.5% ACoS. Recommendation. Evaluate whether adding an audience-retargeting layer fits the account objective and available audience scale, and hold Offensive spend to its efficient competitor terms rather than broad conquesting.
