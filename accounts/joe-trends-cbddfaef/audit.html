Recommendations

Efficiency Execute Priority: High

Observation. Account ACoS is 63.3% against a 30% target, 33.3 points over.

Diagnosis. Spend went from $1,987 in Aug 2026 to $9,169 in the last 30 days; the added budget landed in Auto and Product Pages, where ACoS runs 73.9% and 176.9%.

Recommendation. Cut Product Page bids, negate the zero-order clusters, and move the converting Auto queries into Exact before adding any budget.

TACoS vs Objective Client Conversation Priority: High

Observation. TACoS is 28.7% against a 15% target, on $31,964 total sales in the last 30 days.

Diagnosis. Ad spend now equals 28.7% of every sales dollar; total sales grew, but advertising cost grew faster.

Recommendation. Bring ACoS back toward 30% first; TACoS follows once Auto and Product Page waste is removed.

Keyword Harvesting Execute Priority: High

Observation. Automatic targeting is 78.2% of SP spend and 67.0% of SP sales at 73.9% ACoS. 108 converting Auto queries ($9,707 in sales) have no Exact-match target.

Diagnosis. Amazon decides who sees the ads for most of the budget; only 2 Exact keywords are live and neither has orders.

Recommendation. Harvest the 108 converting Auto queries into Exact and Phrase campaigns with their own bids, and negate them in Auto so the two don't compete.

Brand Defense Execute Priority: High

Observation. Branded spend is $1,633, 17.8% of SP spend, driving $4,788 in ad sales at 34.1% ACoS. 10 branded queries have no Exact-match target, including "swing speed radar" (508 searches, 83.33% purchase share).

Diagnosis. Brand traffic is covered by Broad match only, so competitors can bid on the same searches at Top of Search.

Recommendation. Add an Exact branded campaign for the 10 gap queries and capture SERP screenshots on "swing speed radar" to check competitor placements.

Search Opportunities Execute Priority: High

Observation. 1 non-branded query clears the signal filter: "speed radar", 3,368 searches, 7.69% purchase share, converting in Auto at 9.0% ACoS with no Exact target.

Diagnosis. The brand's non-branded demand is thin in the SQP data; the queries that do convert are reached only through Auto and Broad.

Recommendation. Build Exact for "speed radar" now and re-run SQP monthly as spend scales.

Negative Keyword Review Execute Priority: Medium

Observation. 66 word patterns in search terms meet the floor with zero orders or ACoS above 60%. "golf gloves men" spent $586 with 0 orders; "launch monitor" spent $682 at 545.3% ACoS.

Diagnosis. Auto is matching the radar to adjacent golf products shoppers are not buying it for.

Recommendation. Add negatives for the zero-order patterns (gloves, men) and test "launch monitor" in its own Exact campaign at a lower bid before negating it.

Placement Allocation Execute Priority: Medium

Observation. Top of Search takes 39.9% of spend and drives 62.7% of ad sales at 40.3% ACoS. Product Pages take 34.6% of spend for 12.4% of ad sales at 176.9% ACoS.

Diagnosis. Over a third of the budget buys product-page clicks that convert at 1.2%, against 5.92% at Top of Search.

Recommendation. Lower Product Page bids on the Auto campaigns and shift that budget to Top of Search modifiers on the converting keywords.

Budget Opportunity Client Conversation Priority: Medium

Observation. All 13 enabled SP campaigns ran out of budget before the end of the day for part of the window (in budget under 85% of the time). Amazon estimates $1,599-$4,847 in missed sales on 3 of them.

Diagnosis. The largest, S-SSR-KW-BOOST-MANUAL-B00124P7SI, was in budget 52.3% of the time on $6,458 spend at 53.8% ACoS — more budget at today's efficiency raises ACoS further.

Recommendation. Fix bids and negatives first, then raise budgets on the 3 campaigns Amazon flags, in order of Estimated Missed Sales.

Strategic Intent Mix Execute Priority: Low

Observation. Prospecting (Auto) carries 78.2% of spend at 73.9% ACoS; Brand Defense is 17.8% at 34.1%. No competitor targeting or retargeting is running.

Diagnosis. The mix has no deliberate offensive layer — competitor ASINs and category pages are reached only when Auto happens to land there.

Recommendation. Add a small competitor and category Product Targeting test once Auto spend is brought down.
